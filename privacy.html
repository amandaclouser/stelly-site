Privacy policy

Stelly for iPhone · Effective November 17, 2026

HomeSupportPrivacy

The short version: Stelly has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Everything you add stays on your phone. The only data that leaves it is the searches and lookups the app makes to public catalogs so it can show you shows, books, music, podcasts and games, and anything you choose to send us yourself.

What Stelly stores, and where

Your library (the shows, movies, books, albums, songs, podcasts and teams you add), your watched and read history, ratings, notes, tags, transcripts, summaries, follows and settings are stored in a database on your iPhone. We never see it. Deleting the app deletes it. A backup you export is a file you keep wherever you save it; we have no copy.

If you add a Claude API key for summaries, it's stored in your phone's keychain and sent only to Anthropic with your requests.

Data we collect

None. We don't collect, store or sell any personal data, and the app contains no analytics or tracking code. There is no account to create.

Requests to other services

To show you catalog information, Stelly sends requests directly from your phone to these services. Each receives what any website visit would: the words you searched or the ID of the item, and your phone's IP address. None of them receive your name, your library or anything you've written. Their own privacy policies apply to what they keep.

ServiceUsed for
TVmazeTV show search, episodes, air dates
TMDBMovies and people search, ratings, cast, where to watch (JustWatch data), images
Open Library (Internet Archive)Book search, authors, series, covers
Apple (iTunes Search API)Books' release dates, music, podcasts and their episode links
MusicBrainz and Cover Art ArchiveAnnounced releases from artists you follow, their covers
Podcast publishersEach podcast's own feed, audio and transcript files, fetched from the publisher's servers
ESPNSports schedules, scores, standings (Sports tab only, when you turn it on)
AnthropicOnly if you add your own Claude API key: the transcript text you ask to summarise

Transcription and Apple Intelligence summaries run on your phone with Apple's on-device models; no audio or text is uploaded for them.

Play, Open and View links hand you to apps and websites you choose (Apple Music, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Overcast, YouTube, IMDb, TVmaze, TMDB, Open Library, ESPN). What happens there is governed by those services.

Goodreads import

The Goodreads export file you pick is read on your phone to fill your library. Titles and authors from it are looked up in Open Library as described above. The file itself is not uploaded anywhere. Stelly is not connected to Goodreads.

Notifications

Reminders about new episodes, releases and games are scheduled locally on your phone. We run no notification server and can't see whether you turned them on.

Tips

Tips are optional in-app purchases handled by Apple. Apple processes the payment and we receive no name, address or payment details, only anonymous totals in our sales reports.

When you contact us

If you email us or use Send feedback, we receive your email address, what you wrote and, if you include it, a diagnostics report (timings of what the app did; it contains no titles from your library unless you paste them). We use it only to reply and fix the problem, and keep it no longer than needed.

Children

Stelly is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Changes that matter will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
