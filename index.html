Stelly

Keep track of what you watch, read and listen to.

HomeSupportPrivacy

Stelly is an iPhone app for TV shows, movies, books, music, podcasts and sports, all in one place: what you're in the middle of, what's coming up, and your own notes and ratings.

  • TV shows: mark episodes watched, see what's next and when it airs, keep notes per episode, rewatch with your old notes beside you.
  • Movies: a watch list, every time you watched something, cast with faces, and the people you follow.
  • Books: reading, want to read, read; reading goals with a history; Goodreads import; books that became movies or shows.
  • Music: rate songs on your own criteria, rank albums and artists, see new releases from artists you follow.
  • Podcasts: new episodes, notes, and transcripts and summaries made on your phone.
  • Sports: follow teams and leagues; upcoming games, live scores and standings.

No account. No ads. No subscription. Your library stays on your phone.

Stelly is free, with an optional tip jar inside the app.
