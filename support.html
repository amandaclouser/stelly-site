Common questions

Where does the data come from?

TV shows and episodes from TVmaze; ratings, streaming availability, cast and movies from TMDB (streaming data by JustWatch); books from Open Library and Apple Books; music and podcasts from Apple's catalog, with announced releases from MusicBrainz; podcast episodes from each podcast's own feed; sports schedules and scores from ESPN. Stelly has no server of its own.

A show, movie or book isn't found

Search by the exact title first. Shows need a TVmaze listing; books are matched by title and author's last name. If something is missing from those catalogs, Stelly can't add it yet.

Where to watch is empty or wrong

Streaming availability comes from JustWatch through TMDB for your phone's region, and covers subscription, free and ad-supported services (not rent or buy). It can lag a few days behind a service's catalog.

Notifications

Stelly asks for permission only when a notification becomes useful: following a show's next episode, a book or album release, or a team's game. Everything is scheduled on the phone; nothing is sent from a server. Manage them in iOS Settings › Notifications › Stelly.

Podcast transcripts and summaries

Transcribing on your phone needs iOS 26 and a few minutes with the app open. Summaries use Apple Intelligence on the phone, or your own Claude API key if you add one in Settings. Spotify and other apps open through links; nothing plays inside Stelly.

Sports tab

It's off by default. Turn it on in Settings › Tabs. Scores and schedules come from ESPN's public site and may occasionally be delayed or unavailable.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Settings › Backup exports one file with your whole library; keep it in Files or iCloud Drive. Import it on the new phone. Items already there are updated, nothing is deleted.

Deleting your data

Stelly stores everything on your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no account to close and nothing held anywhere else.

Something is slow

Settings › Diagnostics shows what the app spent its time on. Send it with your feedback and say which screen it was.
